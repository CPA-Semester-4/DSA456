# AVL Trees

AVL trees maintain <i><b>height balanced</b></i> binary search tree.
- <b>Height balanced</b> BST is either `empty` or the height of the left and right subtree differ by no more than 1.
- Search/Insertion/Deletion in a height balanced tree can be done in `O(log n)` time.
- Augmentation - height of every subtree is stored in it's root.
- Balance Calculation: `height of right subree` - `height of the left subree`
- No node's balance should end up anything other than `-1, 0, or 1.`
